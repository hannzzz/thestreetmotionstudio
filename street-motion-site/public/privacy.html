Privacy notice

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who we are

Street Motion Studio is a creative studio based in Bristol, UK. We are responsible for the personal information described in this notice. You can contact us at hannan@thestreetmotionstudio.com.

What we collect

  • When you use our contact form: your name, business name, email address, phone number (if you give it), your website or social media link, and your message.
  • When you email us: your email address and anything you include in your message.
  • If we contacted you first: business contact details that are publicly available, such as a business email address, website and social media pages, so that we can offer you free sample content.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to you, to prepare samples and quotes, to deliver work you ask for, and to keep basic records. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in running and promoting our business, or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. We never sell your information.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run our business: Netlify (website hosting and form submissions) and our email provider. If you become a client, we may also use payment and accounting services. Some of these providers may process data outside the UK under appropriate safeguards.

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with them. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for up to 12 months unless you become a client. Client records are kept for as long as needed for tax and accounting purposes, usually six years.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or object to us using it, including asking us never to contact you again. Just email us and we'll act on it promptly. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
